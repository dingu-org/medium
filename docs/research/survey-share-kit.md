# Survey share kit

Ready-to-post copy for spreading the survey. Replace `LINK` with your tagged Tally URL for each channel (see the `?source=` scheme in [the survey spec](./medium-validation-survey.md#channel-attribution)). Albanian is primary; use the English versions for international/EN channels.

**Tone:** honest founder validation, not a hard sell. "I'm building this, help me shape it" converts better with professionals and is truthful — nothing is launched yet.

---

## 1. WhatsApp / direct message (short)

**SQ:**

> Përshëndetje! Po ndërtoj një asistent që u përgjigjet klientëve në WhatsApp dhe u rezervon takimet vetë. Po mbledh mendime nga profesionistë para se ta nis — 5 minuta, anonim. Do të kisha shumë qejf përgjigjen tënde 🙏 LINK

**EN:**

> Hi! I'm building an assistant that replies to clients on WhatsApp and books their appointments automatically. I'm gathering input from professionals before launch — 5 min, anonymous. Would really value your take 🙏 LINK

## 3. Profession Facebook/community groups (respectful, value-first)

Post in groups for physiotherapists, dentists, trainers, aesthetics, etc. Read each group's self-promo rules first.

**SQ:**

> Përshëndetje të gjithëve 👋 Po punoj për një mjet që u përgjigjet klientëve në WhatsApp dhe u cakton takimet automatikisht, veçanërisht për profesionistë që punojnë vetëm. Not selling anything — just trying to learn whether it solves a real problem and what it'd be worth to you. Anonymous, 5-minute survey. Thanks for your time 🙏 LINK

- [ ] (EN) Reddit r/physicaltherapy, indie-founder / small-biz forums → `?source=reddit`
- [ ] (EN) LinkedIn post → `?source=linkedin`

## Tips for a good sample

- **Aim for 30–50+ responses per key profession** before reading pricing seriously — Van Westendorp needs a reasonable base.
- **Post in the morning or early evening**, when solo professionals check their phones between clients.
- **Reply to comments** — engagement lifts reach and shows you're a real person, which raises completion.
- **Don't incentivize with money** (it skews willingness-to-pay answers). If you must, offer _early/free access_ — which also grows your waitlist.
- Watch the `source` field: double down on whichever channel actually converts.
