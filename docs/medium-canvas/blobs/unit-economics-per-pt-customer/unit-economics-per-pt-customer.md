# Unit economics (per PT customer)

**Monthly costs to serve one PT (direct WhatsApp API):**
- WhatsApp fees: ~€1-5 (mostly €0 for patient-initiated, €0.01-0.10 for reminders)
- AI inference: ~€5-15 (100 conversations @ €0.05-0.15 per conversation)
- Infrastructure: ~€5-10 (hosting, database, webhook processing)
**Total: €11-30/month per PT**

**Market pricing:** PT tools charge €30-150/month. AI assistants charge €49-299/month. Sweet spot appears to be **€49-79/month** for solo practitioners.

**Margin:** At €59/month pricing, gross margin ~50-80% per PT. Economics improve as AI costs decrease and infrastructure scales.
